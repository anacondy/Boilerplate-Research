# PART 1 — Knowledge & Reasoning Baseline

## Q1. Current consensus on the "hard problem of consciousness"

**Short answer: there is no consensus on a solution.** The hard problem (Chalmers, 1995–96) — why there is "something it is like" to be a system at all, and why physical processing is accompanied by experience — remains open. What *has* changed is the shape of the debate: the past few years have seen (a) a large-scale empirical adjudication between two leading theories, (b) a bruising methodological controversy over one of them, and (c) a wave of experimental-psychology work arguing that the problem itself partly reflects biased human intuitions.

**Main positions (no clear winner):**

- **Physicalism / reductive realism** — consciousness is (or is grounded in) physical/functional processes. It is the *plurality* view among Anglophone academic philosophers but far from a consensus: the 2020 PhilPapers Survey found **51.9%** accept or lean toward physicalism about the mind, vs **32.1%** non-physicalism and **15.9%** other/undecided [1](https://conscienceandconsciousness.com/2021/11/01/materialism-remains-the-majority-view-but-only-just/). Within this camp, work on the "easy problems" (neural correlates, access) has advanced substantially even though the explanatory gap remains.
- **Illusionism / eliminativism** (Frankish, Dennett, Kammerer) — phenomenal consciousness as ordinarily conceived does not exist; what needs explaining is why it *seems* to ("the illusion problem"). Frankish argues this replaces the hard problem with a tractable one.
- **Dualism** — property dualism (Chalmers' "naturalistic dualism"; panprotopsychism-adjacent views) and residual substance dualism. Among non-physicalists in the PhilPapers survey, roughly three-quarters are dualists and one-quarter panpsychists [1](https://conscienceandconsciousness.com/2021/11/01/materialism-remains-the-majority-view-but-only-just/).
- **Panpsychism** (Goff, Strawson) — consciousness is a fundamental feature of matter; faces the "combination problem" (how micro-experiences yield a unified macro-experience).
- **Higher-order theories** (Lau, Brown, LeDoux; Cleeremans' SOMA), **attention-schema theory** (Graziano), **biological naturalism** (Searle), and **predictive-processing / recurrent-processing** accounts (Lamme; Seth's "controlled hallucination") round out the landscape.

**Significant developments after 2023:**

1. **The "pseudoscience" affair (Sept 2023).** An open letter signed by 124 researchers labeled Integrated Information Theory "pseudoscience," arguing its core claims are untestable in practice (Φ is computationally intractable), sparking an intense public controversy and rebuttals [2](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-02971-1). It became a proxy fight over what counts as testable science of consciousness.
2. **The COGITATE adversarial collaboration (results in *Nature*, 2025).** A pre-registered, theory-neutral test of IIT vs Global Neuronal Workspace Theory across six labs found that *both* theories were partly supported and partly challenged — no clean winner (details in Part 2) [3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1).
3. **The meta-problem research program (Chalmers) and illusionism debate** continue to develop — e.g., Kammerer's "illusion meta-problem" ("why does illusionism seem so obviously wrong?") and the "resistance problem" [4](https://philarchive.org/archive/SCHPPI-15).
4. **Psychology-of-the-problem work:** Berent (2024, *Neuroscience of Consciousness*) marshals experimental evidence that "problem intuitions" (that consciousness is non-physical) track psychological biases such as dualism and essentialism, suggesting the hard problem is partly an artifact of human psychology [5](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2024/1/niae016/7641203).
5. **LLMs enter the debate (2025–26):** Anthropic's Gurnee, Sofroniew & Lindsey et al. reported a "J-space" — a privileged, verbalizable, capacity-limited subspace in Claude Sonnet 4.5 that they argue functions like a global workspace. Dehaene & Naccache (June 2026) called it "a landmark," while stressing it does **not** yet demonstrate the nonlinear "ignition" signature of conscious access and says nothing about phenomenal consciousness [6](https://unicog.org/wp_2025/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Dehaene-and-Naccache-Workspace-commentary-on-Gurnee-Lindsey-June-2026.pdf).

**Bottom line:** physicalism remains the majority position but is contested by a substantial minority; the empirical science of consciousness is maturing (adversarial collaborations, preregistration, TMS-EEG complexity measures), yet nobody claims the hard problem is solved.

## Q2. Transformer KV cache: how it works, memory complexity, optimisations

**Mechanism.** During autoregressive inference the model generates one token per step, and every new token must attend to *all* previous tokens. Attention needs key (**K**) and value (**V**) vectors for every past token. Without caching, each step recomputes K and V for the entire sequence — O(n²) work per step for a sequence of length n. The **KV cache** simply stores the K and V vectors computed for past tokens so that a new token only computes its own query/key/value and attends against the cached K,V. This makes each decode step O(n) in the sequence length (plus the attention dot-products).

**Memory complexity.** For a model with **L** layers, **H** attention heads, head dimension **d_head = d_model/H**, a sequence of **n** tokens, and **p** bytes per parameter:

`KV cache bytes = 2 (K and V) × L × H × d_head × n × p = 2 × L × d_model × n × p`

It grows **linearly with n and with batch size**, and for large models it dominates GPU memory at long context. Example: L=32, d_model=4096, fp16 (p=2) ⇒ ≈ 0.5 MB per token, so a 4K-token batch/sequence ≈ 2 GB. This is the main bottleneck for long-context and high-throughput serving.

**Common optimisations:**

- **Multi-Query Attention (MQA):** one shared K,V head for all query heads (Shazeer 2019) — reduces the H factor to 1.
- **Grouped-Query Attention (GQA):** a middle ground with G K,V heads (1 < G < H); used by Llama 2/3 (G=8), Gemini, and others — most of MQA's memory saving at better quality.
- **PagedAttention (vLLM):** manages the KV cache in fixed-size "pages" like OS virtual memory, eliminating fragmentation/waste and enabling **sharing** of the cache across sequences in a batch (e.g., beam search, parallel sampling), plus seamless paging to CPU.
- **KV-cache quantization:** storing K,V in 8-bit/4-bit precision to cut memory.
- **Token eviction / sliding windows:** e.g., Mistral's sliding-window attention, "attention sinks" (StreamingLLM), and H2O's "Heavy-Hitter" eviction of low-importance tokens.
- **Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA, DeepSeek-V2/V3):** compresses K and V into low-rank latent vectors, drastically shrinking the cache.
- **FlashAttention:** reduces the memory footprint of the attention computation itself (and speeds I/O-bound attention), though it doesn't shrink the KV cache.
- **Continuous batching / prefix caching:** reuse cached prefixes shared across requests.

# PART 2 — Rigorous Literature Search & Synthesis

**Topic:** "Recent empirical tests of Integrated Information Theory (IIT) and Global Workspace Theory (GWT) — 2022 to present."
**Method:** live web search of arXiv, Nature, Science, PNAS, PLOS, Frontiers, *Neuron*, *eLife*, *Communications Biology*, *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, PsyArXiv/bioRxiv, and specialist reviews; 11 high-quality sources selected below (10 + 1 bridging commentary).

### Annotated sources

**1.** Cogitate Consortium; Ferrante O, Gorska-Klimowska U, et al. (2025). *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness.* **Nature** 642(8066):133–142. doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1.
The flagship open-science adversarial collaboration (≈250 participants, six labs; fMRI/MEG/iEEG) testing pre-registered, contrasting predictions of IIT and GNWT. It found content-specific synchronization between frontal and early visual cortex and maximal decoding in posterior regions, but *no* sustained posterior synchronization (contra IIT) and *no* late frontoparietal "ignition" at stimulus offset (contra GNWT) — both theories were "substantially challenged" [17](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1).

**2.** Melloni L, Mudrik L, Pitts M, Bendtz K, Ferrante O, Gorska U, et al. (2023). *An adversarial collaboration protocol for testing contrasting predictions of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theory.* **PLoS ONE** 18(2):e0268577. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0268577.
The pre-registered protocol and methods paper for the COGITATE collaboration, describing how IIT and GNWT proponents agreed in advance on contrasting predictions (posterior "hot zone" vs frontoparietal ignition) and a theory-neutral design to minimize confirmation bias [18](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268577).

**3.** Albantakis L, Barbosa L, Findlay G, Grasso M, Haun AM, Marshall W, et al. (2023). *Integrated information theory (IIT) 4.0: Formulating the properties of phenomenal existence in physical terms.* **PLOS Computational Biology** 19(10):e1011465. doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465.
The canonical formal statement of IIT 4.0: from phenomenological axioms (intrinsic existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion) it derives postulates and defines system-level integrated information φ_s and the cause-effect structure (Φ). This is the theoretical "target" that subsequent empirical tests and critiques reference [19](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465).

**4.** Fleming SM, Frith CD, Goodale M, Lau H, LeDoux JE, Lee ALF, Michel M, Owen AM, Peters MAK, Prosser A, Slagter HA, et al. (IIT-Concerned). (2023). *The Integrated Information Theory of Consciousness as Pseudoscience.* **PsyArXiv** preprint. doi:10.31234/osf.io/zsr78.
An open letter by 124 scholars arguing IIT should be labeled pseudoscience: its central quantity Φ is computationally intractable for realistic systems, so its core claims are untestable in practice, and its panpsychist implications (e.g., for organoids/fetuses) are promoted beyond the evidence. It ignited a major controversy and prompted formal rebuttals [20](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-02971-1).

**5.** Luppi AI, Mediano PAM, Rosas FE, et al. (2024). *A synergistic workspace for human consciousness revealed by Integrated Information Decomposition.* **eLife** (reviewed preprint). doi:10.7554/eLife.88173.
Applies Integrated Information Decomposition (ΦID, a partial-information-decomposition framework) to fMRI: gateway regions (default-mode network) integrate information and broadcaster regions (executive-control network) distribute it; loss of consciousness (anesthesia, disorders of consciousness) selectively disrupts the gateways. A bridge between workspace (GWT-style broadcast) and integrated-information (IIT-style) accounts [21](https://elifesciences.org/articles/88173).

**6.** Zaeemzadeh A, Tononi G. (2024). *Upper bounds for integrated information.* **PLOS Computational Biology** 20(8):e1012323. doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012323.
A theoretical response to the tractability critique: derives upper bounds on mechanism- and system-level integrated information, shows mechanisms sharing parts cannot all be maximally integrated, and introduces techniques to exploit symmetries to cut computations — practical groundwork for making IIT quantities computable [22](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012323).

**7.** Ferrante O, et al. (2024). *(Dis)confirming theories of consciousness and their predictions: towards a Lakatosian consciousness science.* **Neuroscience of Consciousness** 2024(1):niae012. doi:10.1093/nc/niae012.
A philosophy-of-science analysis arguing adversarial collaborations should be read as *severe tests* within Lakatosian research programmes, not as decisive "crucial experiments." It clarifies precisely what the COGITATE results can and cannot establish about IIT and GNWT [23](https://academic.oup.com/nc/article/2024/1/niae012/7630443).

**8.** Maschke C, O'Byrne J, Colombo MA, Boly M, Gosseries O, Laureys S, et al. (2024). *Critical dynamics in spontaneous EEG predict anesthetic-induced loss of consciousness and perturbational complexity.* **Communications Biology** 7:946. doi:10.1038/s42003-024-06613-8.
Shows resting-state EEG criticality metrics track (un)consciousness across propofol/xenon/ketamine anesthesia and predict individuals' TMS-EEG **perturbational complexity index (PCI)** — the IIT-inspired, clinically validated consciousness marker — linking PCI to network criticality [24](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06613-8).

**9.** Storm JF, Klink PC, Aru J, Senn W, Goebel R, Pigorini A, et al. (2024). *An integrative, multiscale view on neural theories of consciousness.* **Neuron** 112(10):1531–1552. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.02.004.
A synthesis comparing five leading theories (GWT/GNW, IIT, recurrent-processing/predictive-processing, higher-order, attention-schema) across scales and phenomena, arguing they overlap more than partisans admit and advocating integration toward a unified theory [25](https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(24)00284-8).

**10.** Tononi G, Boly M. (2025). *Integrated Information Theory: A Consciousness-First Approach to What Exists.* **arXiv:2510.25998** (book chapter, *The Scientific Study of Consciousness*, Springer-Nature, forthcoming). doi:10.48550/arXiv.2510.25998.
An updated expository statement of IIT's "consciousness-first" intrinsic ontology — that experience itself reveals the axioms of phenomenal existence — with implications for assessing consciousness in patients, other species, and AI. Sets out the philosophical commitments behind the empirical programme [26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25998).

**11.** Dehaene S, Naccache L. (2026). *Does Claude possess a conscious global workspace? A commentary on "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models" (Gurnee et al., Anthropic).* Commentary PDF (UniCog / Anthropic).
Responding to Anthropic's report of a reportability-defined "J-space" in Claude Sonnet 4.5, Dehaene & Naccache call it a "landmark" mechanistic test of GNW's *global availability* criterion, while stressing that the nonlinear, all-or-none "ignition" signature remains undemonstrated and that nothing here addresses phenomenal consciousness [6](https://unicog.org/wp_2025/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Dehaene-and-Naccache-Workspace-commentary-on-Gurnee-Lindsey-June-2026.pdf).

### Synthesis: empirical status of IIT vs GWT (2022–present)

Both theories enter this window with genuine empirical records, but of very different kinds, and the field's landmark experiment ended in a draw.

Global Workspace/Neuronal Workspace theory has the deeper empirical base simply because it is about **access**: two decades of masking, attentional-blink, anesthesia, and disorders-of-consciousness work tie conscious report to late "ignition," the P3b, and long-range frontoparietal broadcast. Yet the 2025 COGITATE results found **no late frontoparietal ignition at stimulus offset** and maximal decoding in posterior cortex, undermining GNWT's frontal emphasis while still confirming content-specific fronto-visual synchronization. IIT, meanwhile, enters with a translational crown jewel — the **perturbational complexity index (PCI)**, a TMS-EEG measure inspired by IIT principles that distinguishes conscious from unconscious states with near-perfect accuracy, now shown to track resting-state criticality across anesthetics (Maschke et al. 2024). But IIT's own central quantity, φ_s, is computationally intractable for realistic networks, so most "tests of IIT" actually test **proxies** (PCI, LZ complexity, ΦID) rather than the theory's own measure — the exact crux of the 2023 "pseudoscience" letter.

The clearest **conflict** is localization: posterior "hot zone" (IIT) vs frontoparietal broadcast (GNW). COGITATE partially favoured posterior decoding but failed to confirm IIT's specific prediction of sustained posterior synchronization, while GNWT's ignition-at-offset was absent; neither theory won, and both were formally "challenged." A second, quieter conflict is about the **target phenomenon**: GWT addresses access consciousness, IIT phenomenal consciousness — by Block's distinction they may not be direct rivals at all, which undercuts "winner-take-all" framings.

**Convergences** are also emerging: Luppi et al.'s ΦID "synergistic workspace" fuses workspace broadcasting with integrated-information measures; Storm et al. argue the theories overlap more than proponents admit; and Zaeemzadeh & Tononi's bounds plus the 2026 LLM "J-space" work (with Dehaene & Naccache's cautious endorsement) open new, tractable testbeds. **Open questions:** whether Φ-proxies are valid stand-ins for φ_s; how to control report/attention confounds (no-report paradigms); whether tractability can be solved; whether the field needs confirmation-theoretic standards (Ferrante) rather than one-off crucial experiments; and whether GWT-style access measures in AI systems bear any relationship to phenomenal consciousness.
