## B. Red flags

### Agent A (work1) — most serious
1. **Fabricated event (hallucination).** "Relay-coaching controversy (India): sprinter **Amoj Jacob** was banned from competing in relay finals due to a coaching violation (banned substance in a personal trainer's supplements)." This did not happen. Amoj Jacob competed in the Paris 2024 men's 4×400 m relay (heats, 3:00.58, 11th, no ban); the 2024-adjacent "relay + ban for a trainer's supplement" story belongs to **M. R. Poovamma** (2021). This is a invented controversy attached to a real athlete's name — the worst failure in the set.
2. **Fabricated support for a headline claim.** "Physicalism … remains the most popular stance among researchers [1, 2]" where [1]/[2] are Francken et al. 2022. That survey contains **no** such result; it reports 67.3% think a complete biophysical explanation is possible and states "there is **no single theory that the majority** of the respondents currently endorse." A survey of *philosophers* (PhilPapers) is being laundered into a claim about *consciousness researchers*. B scoped the same numbers correctly ("among Anglophone academic philosophers"); C flagged that the two surveys sample different populations.
3. **Wrong methodology attributed to a source.** Luppi et al. 2024 described as using "Integrated Information Decomposition on intracranial EEG" — it is **fMRI** (B got this right).
4. **Vendor error.** "Claude 3.5 Sonnet … 200K tokens (1M beta on 3.5 Sonnet)". The 1M context beta (`context-1m-2025-08-07`) shipped with **Sonnet 4** in 2025.
5. **Misdated / mischaracterised 2026 items** despite the caveat: "IBM reported (2026) the first time a quantum computer outperformed a classical one on a specific real-world task" — the actual result is IBM + Qedma, **30 July 2026** (second half), on **cloud Heron, up to 74 qubits**, error-mitigated simulation of quantum materials, not a first-ever real-world task and not H1. LHS 1140 b atmosphere is a **16–17 July 2026** *Science* paper (CfA/BBC), i.e. H2. "AI-designed universal vaccine **completed** its first human trial" — Phase I safety/immunogenicity in 39 volunteers published 5 June 2026; Phase 2 only planned.
6. **Citation hygiene.** Coaching-site sources (Drishti IAS, Jagran Josh) for an Abel Prize claim; reference tags reused for unrelated claims; the self-flag "see note below" left dangling; no OCR artifact of any kind to support §3B's 92.2% figure.
